Privacy policy

Hasim – Green Hydrogen Engineering · Microsoft Store edition and free evaluation · version 2.1.0 · 2026-09-25

Summary

Hasim runs on your computer. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, and it contains no advertising and no usage analytics.

Your projects

Design bases, projects, component libraries and settings are stored only on your computer – in the storage of the app and in the files you save yourself. Hasim does not send them anywhere.

Program files

Hasim from the Microsoft Store contains all its program files and manuals and works without an internet connection; it only opens web pages or e-mail when you click a link. The free evaluation at https://haymohsen.github.io/Hasim/ loads its program files from GitHub Pages and keeps a copy for offline use. Like any web server, GitHub may record technical access data such as IP addresses; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Purchases

Hasim is sold by Microsoft in the Microsoft Store as a one-time purchase; payment details are handled by Microsoft and never reach Hasim. Hasim recognises that it runs as the app installed from the Microsoft Store; it stores nothing about your purchase or your Microsoft account. Licence keys bought from the author are checked offline on your computer.
